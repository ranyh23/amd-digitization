About the project

A systematic effort to open
historical physician records

The American Medical Directory (AMD) was published by the American Medical Association beginning in 1906. Each volume listed licensed physicians across the United States, recording name, location, medical school, year of graduation, and other professional details. The directory is a unique longitudinal source for the study of the medical profession in the early twentieth century.

This project makes these volumes available as structured, machine-readable data for use in historical and social science research. All data is freely downloadable and accompanied by documentation.

Background

The American Medical Directory

The American Medical Directory was published by the American Medical Association from 1906 onward. It constitutes one of the most comprehensive contemporaneous records of licensed physicians in the United States, covering name, location, specialty, medical school, and licensure year.

Our goal is to make each available volume accessible as clean, structured data, enabling researchers to study the geography, training, and composition of the American medical profession across the first half of the twentieth century.

All digitization follows strict transcription protocols: data is entered exactly as it appears in the original source, with no inference or imputation. Accompanying documentation describes the source, structure, and any known limitations of each volume.
